When people say "the service dog form," they mean the U.S. DOT Service Animal Air Transportation Form. It is the service-animal form a U.S. airline can require for the cabin.

## What it covers

The form is about your dog's health, training, and behavior in public. You check the boxes and sign it yourself. Nobody else, including PetPassGo, can attest for you.

## Where you get it

The form for your trip is on a service-dog Travel Pass, after you [Plan my trip](/assessment) and pay. It is filled with the pet and account details you already saved. You still review it, check each box, and sign.

## No letter replaces it

No internet letter, registry, or certificate stands in for the DOT form. [A psychiatric service dog does not need a PSD letter.](/blog/do-you-need-a-psd-letter)
