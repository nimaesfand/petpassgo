**Short answer: no.** For a flight on a U.S. airline, a psychiatric service dog does not need a PSD letter, a registry ID, or a paid certificate.

## What this means

A psychiatric service dog is a service dog. The airline may ask for the official U.S. DOT service-animal form. A letter from a website does not replace that form, and an ESA letter does not turn an animal into a service dog.

If your dog is not task-trained, it can fly as a pet. [A service dog, a PSD, and an ESA are not the same thing at the airport.](/blog/service-dog-vs-esa-vs-psd-flying)
