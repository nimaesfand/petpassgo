## Promise of the piece

Help a traveler tell three labels apart before they buy the wrong letter.

## Outline

1. Service dog: a dog trained to do work for a person with a disability. U.S. airlines follow the DOT definition. Any breed.
2. PSD means psychiatric service dog. A PSD is a service dog. Same rules, same official DOT form. It is not a different species of paperwork.
3. ESA means emotional support animal. Since 2021, U.S. airlines do not treat an ESA as a service animal. An ESA flies as a pet: carrier under the seat, and the airline's pet fee.
4. What PetPassGo will not do: we do not sell, sign, or attest a service-animal form. We do not ask anyone to buy a certificate or a letter. The paid pass uses the official DOT PDFs only.
5. If the animal is not a dog, it is not a U.S. air-travel service animal. Offer the pet path. Do not dead-end the reader.
6. Alaska and JetBlue "Service Animal ID" (if the published guide mentions it): free, issued by the airline, not a product you purchase.
