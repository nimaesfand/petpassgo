## Promise of the piece

Explain the one official form, and the 48-hour rule without making it sound automatic.

## Outline

1. The form travelers mean is the U.S. DOT Service Animal Air Transportation Form. Flights of 8 hours or more may also need the DOT Relief Attestation.
2. Deadline wording to keep: an airline may require the form up to 48 hours before departure if the ticket was purchased more than 48 hours in advance. It is not an unconditional "always 48 hours" rule. Cite 14 CFR 382.75 when a human verifies the sentence.
3. The traveler checks the attestation boxes and signs. PetPassGo will not attest for them.
4. No internet letter or certificate replaces the DOT form.
5. One task, not four: the form, sending it to the airline, and the reminder are the same job.
