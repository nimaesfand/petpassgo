## Promise of the piece

Answer the question in the title, then point people away from letter mills.

## Outline

1. Direct answer: for a U.S. flight, a psychiatric service dog does not need a PSD letter or a paid certificate. A PSD is a service dog.
2. What the airline can ask for is the official DOT form (and the relief attestation on a long flight), not a letter from a website.
3. An ESA letter does not turn an animal into a service dog. Since 2021 an ESA flies as a pet, with the carrier and the pet fee.
4. PetPassGo will not write, sell, or sign that paperwork.
5. If someone arrived from a search for "buy a PSD letter," say plainly that we don't sell one, and offer the pet path if the animal is not a task-trained service dog.
