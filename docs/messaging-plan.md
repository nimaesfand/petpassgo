Why this exists: Nima wants every line on the site to hit a real moment in a pet owner's life ("ever had this happen? we've got you"), instead of listing features. This plan is built on research into what each kind of customer actually runs into, plus a check of what PetPassGo really does today.

## 2. What the research says, by customer

### Everyday pet owners (the moments Pet Vault is for)
- **Groomers, boarding, daycare and trainers all ask for proof of vaccines.** VCA boarding: "proof of vaccination at check-in or have your veterinarian email the records." Another VCA site: records must arrive 72 hours before a stay, or "VCA has the right to cancel reservations." Another charges for an exam on arrival if records are missing. PetSmart requires rabies paperwork for grooming, hotel and training.
- **Renting with a pet:** landlords commonly ask for vaccination records, a pet resume and microchip info during pet screening.
- **New vet or emergency clinic:** records stay with the old clinic and don't follow the pet. Owners end up guessing at intake.
- **Lost pets:** in the ASPCA's national survey, 15% of owners lost a dog or cat in 5 years, and a tag or microchip led to the return of 15% of recovered dogs. PetHub sells speed ("96% … home in 24 hours or less").
- **Competitor warning:** Waggle Vault stores records, uses AI to organize them, and shares them by link with vets, sitters and boarders, for free. **Records alone will never justify $6.99 a month.** Nima was right.

### Flying with a pet (Travel Pass and the free check)
- Fear for the pet's safety is the top reason owners don't fly with them, followed by worry about behavior and cost.
- About 1 in 5 (21.7%) who flew with a pet had trouble with their airline. Fees run $95–$200, and policies vary by airline and change often (CBS).
- **Moving is the #1 reason pets fly** (about 45–48%). Movers also need records for the new landlord and the new vet. That's one customer with three needs.

### Service dog handlers
- An airline can deny a service dog if it requires the DOT forms and they aren't provided (DOT). News stories show handlers stuck over form timing.
- Handlers face challenges everywhere: 43% of 1,000+ handlers worldwide were refused access somewhere in the past year (ADI/IGDF 2025).
- **Careful:** under the ADA, shops and restaurants can't demand papers. So we sell being ready at the **airport** (where DOT forms are allowed). We never promise "access anywhere".

### ESA owners
- The pain is the landlord: at lease renewal, "exactly the wrong moment to scramble for updated proof". Letters usually need renewing every year.

## 3. Positioning

**Pet Vault isn't a folder of records. It's what gets your pet in the door, wherever you go:** the airport, the groomer, boarding, a new vet, a new apartment. And it's what gets them back if they're ever lost.

Tone: name the moment ("Groomer asking for vaccines?"), then calm it ("Send them in seconds"). Short. No feature names ("travel mode", "Pet ID").

## 5. What has to be true before this copy goes live

- **"Share their vaccines in seconds"**: mostly true today. Pet Vault has a "Care card for sitters": a private link that expires, with a PDF. It's buried under a sitter label. To make the promise strong:
  1. Rename it **"Share records"** and move it to the top of the pet's Vault.
  2. **Nima's idea:** type the groomer's or vet's email and we send it for them (link + PDF), then show "Sent".
- **"One tap at the airport"**: true (travel mode).
- **"One scan of their tag reaches you"**: true. The finder messages you straight from the scan page, and while the pet is marked lost, a scan also emails you (at most once an hour) with the approximate area.
- **Reminders for letters**: true (built today).

## 6. Free pet-record apps: how they make money, and how we beat them (added Nov 25)

| Company | Free part | How they really get paid |
|---|---|---|
| Waggle Vault | Records vault, AI organizing, share link | Pet temperature monitors and cameras with monthly plans. The vault is bait. |
| PetDesk (7M+ owners) | Owner app: records, reminders, booking | Vet clinics pay (about $249+/month per clinic) |
| Pawprint → GreatPetCare | Fetched your records from your vet for you | Later limited the free version (30 days of records) and charged about $5/month. Planned in-app promotions. |
| PetHub | Basic tag profile | Tags plus a premium plan (scan alerts with a map, shelter alerts) |

**Lessons**
1. Records alone are a free commodity. Nobody wins on storage.
2. What owners loved most was **someone else getting the records for them** (Pawprint asked the vet directly).
3. Owners act when something outside forces them to: a boarding reservation or a housing rule, not a vet reminder.
4. Owners rarely use apps to share vaccines with groomers or kennels today. Kennels complain about blurry photos and misread dates. They want proof they can trust.

**How PetPassGo does it better**
- **Verified, not just stored:** our vet-confirm flow means a groomer or kennel sees "confirmed by Dr. X at Y Clinic on date", not a blurry photo.
- **We send it for you:** type the groomer's or kennel's email and we send the verified records (Nima's idea, now backed by research).
- **We get it from your vet:** new idea. Owner types the clinic's email, and we ask the vet to send or confirm the records. This answers "your documents aren't there yet".
- **Everything else free apps don't do:** the passport, airline rules and DOT forms, the lost-pet tag, ESA and service dog letters.
- **Money beyond $6.99:** groomers, kennels and vets who receive our records are natural Discover listing customers later.
